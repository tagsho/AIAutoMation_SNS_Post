Research date: 2026-05-25

## Takeaway

Use opt-in offers as a lower-friction bridge before high-ticket affiliate offers.

Best fit by account:

- Fortune account: fortune consultation registrations, chat/email fortune first-use offers, VERNIS registration/usage flow.
- Side-hustle account: ASP registration, affiliate-start guides, LINE registration/free seminar offers, AI/business free materials.

Avoid making the posts sound like "free only is enough." Treat opt-in as "first step / checklist / consultation entry."

## Rejection Rules

Reject offers that:

- claim guaranteed income
- require fake registration or no-intent registration
- hide advertiser identity
- prohibit SNS traffic
- rely only on "free money" wording
- require aggressive DM spam
- have unclear denial conditions
